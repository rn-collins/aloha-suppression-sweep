Method

Three evidence boundaries

1 · Source identity

Platform statements, oversight decisions, government records, and organization reports are not interchangeable.

2 · Claim strength

“Removed in error” is confirmed only when the platform or authoritative reviewer says so. Everything else keeps its reported or alleged status.

3 · Causation

Timing, reach loss, or correlation can trigger review; none independently proves discriminatory or intentional enforcement.
