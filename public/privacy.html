What stays in your browser

The site has no account, cookies, analytics, or submission endpoint. Save flags and assessments are stored in local storage under platform-evidence-lab-v1 until you use Reset, clear site data, or remove them through your browser.

Exports and external links

JSON export is created locally and includes the public evidence records plus your saved flags and assessments. Opening a cited source sends ordinary request metadata to that third-party site under its own privacy practices. This site uses a strict-origin-when-cross-origin referrer policy.

Use boundaries

Do not enter case narratives, names, identifiers, or other sensitive information. The current interface does not request free-text submissions.
